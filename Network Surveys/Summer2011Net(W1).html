26  Summer2011Net(W1)

In this survey you will be asked questions about your personal contact network. We estimate that it will take around 30 minutes to complete this survey, though for some people it may take a bit longer. You can always save your results and then return to the survey later by clicking on the link in the email we sent you.

The purpose of this survey is to obtain a baseline depiction of your social network prior to your arrival at Notre Dame. This is important information that we will need to understand how social networks change with the transition to college.

All information that your provide to us through this survey is confidential. In all reports and presentations individuals will not be identifiable. Also, no one will see your answers except the researchers involved in the NetSense project.

We know you are very busy this week, but we would very much appreciate it if you could find the time to complete this survey prior to your arrival at Notre Dame.

When taking the survey you can return to previous pages by clicking the “Previous” button at the bottom of a page. Please do not use your browsers “Back” button.

Thank you for taking the time to carefully answer these questions. If you have any questions please email us at netsense@nd.edu.

Information on your contacts In this section you will be asked to provide information about each of your contacts. ### 31 [Phone.num.email]

  1. In the next question, we will ask for several pieces of confidential information about your existing contacts. Specifically, we will be asking for the phone numbers and email addresses that you use to communicate with those contacts. We are asking for this information in order to measure your level of communication activity with these people after you arrive at Notre Dame. As you will recall, we are capturing communication activity from your smart-­phone containing the numbers and email addresses of the people that you speak with, email and text. By linking the information you provide, we will be able to measure the extent to which you communicate with each of these persons. It is important to note that we will not contact any of your contacts. All information will be kept strictly confidential on our secure servers that are only accessible by researchers on the NetSense project team.
