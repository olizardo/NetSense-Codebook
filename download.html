1 Data Download

The cleaned, de-identified datasets from the NetSense study are available for open academic research. All files have been programmatically scrubbed of sensitive personal identifiers (including student names, email addresses, NetIDs, and raw telephone numbers).

1.1 Longitudinal Social Network Surveys

Longitudinal panel tracking dyadic nominations between students across Waves 1–8 (\(N = 11,818\) nominations). Includes subjective closeness, relationship duration, tie categories, contact frequencies, and emotional profiles.

CSV Dataset (.csv, 33 MB) R Dataset (.rds, 600 KB) Stata Dataset (.dta, 89 MB) SPSS Dataset (.sav, 4 MB)

1.2 Perceived Alter-to-Alter Network Ties

Dyadic edge list capturing all perceived acquaintance ties between nominated alters within each participant’s personal network across Waves 1–5, 7, and 8 (\(N = 29,473\) perceived ties).

Alter-Alter Ties (.csv, 1.4 MB) Alter-Alter Ties (.rds, 180 KB) Alter-Alter Ties (.dta, 2.1 MB)

NoteWave 6 Survey Design

Perceived alter-to-alter tie data was deliberately omitted from the Wave 6 (Summer 2013) survey instrument to reduce respondent burden during summer break. Full alter-to-alter batteries were reinstated in Waves 7 and 8.

1.3 Ego-Network Structural Metrics

Longitudinal summary panel (\(N = 804\) ego-waves) tracking personal network size (\(k\)), potential alter pairs (\(\binom{k}{2}\)), reported ties, and personal network density / local clustering coefficients.

Ego Metrics (.csv, 42 KB) Ego Metrics (.rds, 10 KB) Ego Metrics (.dta, 85 KB)

1.4 Longitudinal Demographic & Behavioral Surveys

Wide individual-level panel (\(N = 203\) students, 1,556 variables) tracking demographic backgrounds, Big Five personality inventories, GPAs, lifestyles, sleep habits, musical tastes, and activities across Waves 1–6.

CSV Dataset (.csv, 2.7 MB) R Dataset (.rds, 280 KB) Stata Dataset (.dta, 6.2 MB) SPSS Dataset (.sav, 4.3 MB)

1.5 High-Frequency Quiz Responses (Master)

High-frequency survey check-ins across semesters (\(N = 194\) students, 146 variables) measuring student emotional well-being, health, and campus engagement.

Quiz CSV (.csv, 65 KB) Quiz Stata (.dta, 320 KB)

1.6 Longitudinal Communication Ego Networks (Calls & SMS)

De-identified dyadic communication panel tracking phone calls and text messages across 158 continuous academic weeks (2011–2014, \(N = 383,895\) dyad-week interaction records). Captures communication volumes with both enrolled study peers and outside alters (family, high school friends, etc.) using consistent, irreversible salted pseudonyms.

Dyadic Communication Panel:

Compressed Dyadic Panel (.csv.gz, 2.7 MB) Dyadic Panel (.csv, 11 MB) R Dataset (.rds, 1.8 MB) Stata Dataset (.dta, 12 MB)

Ego-Level Weekly Summary Metrics:

Longitudinal summary metrics (\(N = 19,598\) ego-weeks) tracking active indegree, outdegree, total SMS inflow/outflow, and total voice call inflow/outflow per participant week.

Ego Weekly Metrics (.csv, 860 KB) R Dataset (.rds, 270 KB) Stata Dataset (.dta, 1.0 MB)

1.7 Longitudinal Email Ego Networks

De-identified longitudinal email interaction panel tracking sent and received emails across 158 study weeks (\(N = 676,717\) dyad-week interaction records from over 1 million parsed emails). Email addresses are completely pseudonymized via salted cryptographic hashes and classified into Notre Dame peer/individual, personal webmail, campus listservs, and automated commercial notifications.

Dyadic Email Panel:

Compressed Dyadic Email Panel (.csv.gz, 3.7 MB) Dyadic Email Panel (.csv, 33 MB) R Dataset (.rds, 3.3 MB) Stata Dataset (.dta, 57 MB)

Ego-Level Weekly Summary Metrics:

Weekly summary metrics (\(N = 14,320\) ego-weeks) tracking interpersonal email degree, person-to-person email volumes, Notre Dame peer emails, campus course/club listserv emails, and automated commercial emails.

Ego Weekly Email Metrics (.csv, 520 KB) R Dataset (.rds, 140 KB) Stata Dataset (.dta, 1.3 MB)

1.8 Physical Proximity Networks (Bluetooth)

Aggregated undirected weekly contact networks (\(N = 165,967\) dyad-weeks) based on high-resolution Bluetooth proximity episodes across academic weeks 8–41.

Weekly Contact Networks (.csv, 6.7 MB) Bluetooth-to-EgoID Key (.csv, 3 KB)

1.9 Variable Catalog & Mapping Dictionaries

Comprehensive machine-readable dictionary covering all survey variables, survey questions, and response value labels.

Full Variable Catalog (.csv, 450 KB) Dorm Distances Matrix (.csv, 2 KB) Semester Dates (.csv, 1 KB)

1.10 Communication Telemetry Data

ImportantRestricted Access

Due to institutional review board (IRB) privacy restrictions and massive data volumes (exceeding 25 GB), raw and fine-grained communication telemetry logs (phone call duration logs, SMS message timestamps, and email traffic) cannot be hosted directly for public web download.

Aggregated weekly communication volumes (indegree, outdegree, and weekly text/voice flows) are available in Data/weekly_aggregated_metrics/. Researchers interested in accessing de-identified communication logs for replication purposes should contact the study investigators at netsense@nd.edu.
