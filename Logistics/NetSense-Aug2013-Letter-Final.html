Dear NetSense Participants,

Greetings from the NetSense research team!  We hope that you have all had a wonderful summer and are looking forward to returning to campus this fall (or perhaps in the spring if you are studying abroad).  As you already know, thanks to the generosity of SPRINT we are able to continue the NetSense study for another two years. This email contains basic information on how you can continue in the study, upgrade your phone, and what continuation will entail for you.

In short, you will be able to upgrade your phone when you arrive on campus, or for those of you studying abroad, before or after you leave the country.  In contrast to before when we had to give you phones with a special custom Android build, your new smartphones will be regular (stock) Android phones with the normal Android operating system. All in all, your phone service and general experience should be dramatically better during the next two years. 

Furthermore, the service procedure for the phones will now dramatically change allowing you to simply go to the local Sprint store to fix your phone and reinstall our research application (agent) easily on your own.  The insurance policy is also dramatically better, allowing for reduced costs on screen cracks, lost, or damaged phones. 

Upgrade Procedure

For upgrading your phone, you will have 3 upgrade choices:  

  1. Samsung Galaxy S3 for $100.  The Galaxy S3 is Samsung’s flagship phone for 2012.

  2. Samsung Galaxy S4 for $200.  The Galaxy S4 is Samsung’s flagship phone for 2013.

  3. HTC One for $200.  The One is HTC’s flagship phone for 2013.

 Each of the three phone models have support for LTE (4G) and should have dramatically better WiFi and cellular reception versus the Nexus S 4G.  These are the only three phone models that are eligible for free service as part of the study. 

The fee for the phone takes care of the upgrade cost and the insurance fee (high end insurance) for the duration of the study (through May 2015).  There is no fee beyond the upgrade cost and the plan will be identical to our prior plan (i.e. free voice/text/data).  The upgrade fee is payable at the Sprint store when you upgrade your phone.

Similar to the first two years of the study, you will likely want to buy a case.  You are welcome to purchase a case in advance on Amazon or other on-line vendors.  

 For financial hardship cases, we have a limited supply of Motorola Photons and Galaxy S3’s.  Please contact netsense@nd.edu if you need to apply for financial assistance with your phone upgrade.

There are three options: Upgrade on campus when you return later this month and, for students who are studying abroad in the Fall, upgrade either prior to leaving or upon your return. No matter how you upgrade, you must send us an email at netsense@nd.edu by August 12th notifying us that you want to stay in the NetSense study and upgrade your phone.

Upgrade - On-Campus in the Fall

  1. For those who will be on campus in the fall semester you need to tell us by August 12th via email:

    • That you want to be in the NetSense study another two years

    • Which of the above three phones you would like to have

    • Whether you are applying for financial assistance

    • You must also complete and sign a new consent form. We will be sending out this form to you within the next week.

  2. Go to the local Sprint store located at 2031 South Bend Avenue (i.e. the Sprint store by the Martin’s at Ironwood and SR 23).  The store employees will be notified in advance that each of you are coming to upgrade and you can stop by whenever you arrive on campus. 

  3. You must turn in your old phone to Christine Broadbent in 275 Fitzpatrick (departmental office for the Department of Electrical Engineering).

  4. Once you have acquired your new phone, send netsense@nd.edu a note and we will send you a download link for the agent via a text message.  Similar to prior upgrades, you will simply click the link and install the software. Later in the semester, we will be upgrading the agent to a newer, more power-efficient agent.

Upgrade - Studying Abroad in Fall (before leaving)

If you are studying abroad and wish to upgrade your phone before you leave:

  1. Let us know as soon as possible:

    • your mailing address

    • the closest location (street address) of your nearest Sprint store

    • the phone you would like to upgrade to. 

  2. Once you receive a confirmation from us, please go to your local Sprint store and upgrade your phone. 

  3. Submit your consent form electronically to netsense@nd.edu prior to leaving the country

  4. Once you have acquired your new phone, send netsense@nd.edu a note and we will send you a download link for the agent via a text message.  Similar to prior upgrades, you will simply click the link and install the software. Later in the semester, we will be upgrading the agent to a newer, more power-efficient agent.

  5. Return your old phone in January of 2014 when you return from studying abroad to Christine Broadbent in 275 Fitzpatrick (departmental office for the Department of Eletrical Engineering). 

Keep in mind that your phone will not work internationally and that international roaming should be disabled. You will be liable for any fees incurred while traveling internationally. It is OK to use the phone strictly over WiFi.

Upgrade - Studying Abroad in Fall (after returning)

If you are studying abroad and wish to upgrade after you return:

  1. Send a note to netsense@nd.edu noting that you want to continue to be in the NetSense study and will be upgrading in January. Please also tell us which phone your would like.

  2. Submit your consent form electronically to netsense@nd.edu

  3. Upon your return in January you will be able to go to the local Sprint store to receive your new phone, and then install our agent on your new phone and return your old phone to us.

As noted above, make sure to disable international roaming with your current phone.

 Consent Form

The consent form for the study renewal will be sent in a separate email in the next week. It is quite similar to the original consent form.  As before, we are asking you to (1) use your NetSense phone as your primary communication device, and (2) complete every semester two longer surveys like you have in the past, and possibly short surveys pushed to your phone like we also did. If you do not think you can meet these two conditions, you should not re-enroll in the NetSense Study.

The main changes to the consent form are with respect to data sharing to more clearly delineate that aggregated and anonymized data may be shared with specific university and industrial partners.  Shared data is always anonymized and very carefully monitored in terms of what is shared including strict privacy controls / agreements between Notre Dame and the respective partner.

Service Procedure

Rather than having to go through the netsense@nd.edu to coordinate phone repairs, you can now simply go to the local Sprint store.  This applies whether you are on campus or are at home. 

If your repair involves acquiring a new phone, simply send us an e-mail at netsense@nd.edu and we will send you the agent link to download and install the agent once your have the new phone. 

Bringing Friends Into the Study

Starting in the second week of classes, we will invite a limited number of new people (50 or less) to join the study. You will have the opportunity to nominate any of your Notre Dame friends to join the study. If chosen to be in the study, your friends will receive a new phone (from among the three listed above) and free voice/text/data plans through May of 2015. They will also be able to port in their number from outside of Sprint (a change from when you enrolled). An e-mail will be distributed later outlining the specific details but for now, start thinking if you would like to invite any fellow students to join the study.

New Addition - Laptop Agent

We will also be requesting that you run a lightweight laptop agent in addition to the phone agent.  The laptop agent captures similar data to the phone agent and allows us to assess which sort of network activities (e.g., emailing, web surfing, Facebook updates) you do on your phone versus which activities you do on your laptop.  Further instructions for the laptop agent (which several of you are already running as part of another study) will be distributed during the first week of classes.

You will receive a $5 gift card at the end of each semester for running the laptop agent.

Please feel free to let us know if you have any questions. 

Best regards,

Profs. Striegel, Hachen, Poellabauer, Lizardo

P.S. If you have received this email in error because you have dropped out from the NetSense study, please send an email to netsenE@nd.edu immediately and let us know this.
